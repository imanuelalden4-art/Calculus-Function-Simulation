Educational Guide

Cartesian Plane: A 2D plane formed by the intersection of two perpendicular axes: X (horizontal) and Y (vertical).

Coordinates: Every point is represented as (x, y), where x is the horizontal position and y is the vertical position.

Algebraic Function: A rule that assigns exactly one y-value for each x-value, creating a curve or line on the graph.
